# Universal Bluetooth IR Remote
A compact universal infrared (IR) remote control powered by an ESP32 C3. It connects to your mobile phone via Bluetooth, allowing you to capture IR codes and blast them from your phone.
## Features
- **Wireless Bluetooth Connectivity:** Connects with phone to control.
- **IR Capturing & Emitting:** TSOP38238 receiver to capture signals and an IR LED blasts packages.
- **Rechargeable & Portable:** Powered by a 3.7V LiPo battery.
- **Custom PCB Design:** Built around the smol Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3.
## Hardware Specs
- **Microcontroller:** Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32 C3 (SMD)
- **IR Receiver:** TSOP38238
- **IR Transmitter:** 940nm IR LED driven by a BC547 NPN Transistor
- **Power:** 3.7V LiPo Battery
